Smart-Hire 🚀
---
Smart-Hire is an intelligent hiring platform designed to streamline recruitment by matching the right candidates with the right roles—faster, fairer, and smarter.

Problem❌
---
Recruiters spend hours manually screening resumes.

Many resumes are AI-generated, making it hard to find genuine talent.

Traditional ATS systems often rely only on keyword matching.

Solution✅
---
SmartHire.AI automates recruitment with AI:

Parses resumes (PDF/DOCX) & extracts skills, experience, education.

Matches candidates to job descriptions using AI-based relevance scoring.

Detects AI-generated or fake resumes.

Ranks candidates: Excellent / Good / Average.

Sends automated interview or rejection emails.

Key Features🌟
---
AI Resume Authenticity Check

Automated Candidate Ranking

Zero-touch Recruitment Automation

Saves 15+ hours per hiring cycle

Tech Stack💻
---
Frontend: HTML, CSS, Bootstrap

Backend: Python (Flask)

AI/ML: NLTK, SpaCy, Scikit-learn

Database: SQLite

Automation: SMTP (Gmail API)
